Inside the Book

A peek at a few pages from There's A Dinosaur Knocking At My Door, coming September 2026.

Pages 1–2

It all starts with a knock. A friendly dinosaur — kippah on his head, flowers in hand — shows up ready to celebrate Shabbat. He wants to have Shabbat with me!

Pages 5–6

Oh, he wants to light the candles. The whole family is there — and so is the dinosaur, right in the middle of the celebration where he belongs.

Pages 9–10

He wants to eat some... CHALLAH! The call-and-response rhythm that has made this song a classroom favorite for thirty years comes through on every page.
