About Mimi

Mimi Greisman, M.A. Ed., is a Jewish Childhood and Family Educator and recipient of the Helen Diller Family Award for Excellence in Jewish Education. For more than three decades she has created Jewish programs for families with young children, and her Shabbat adaptation of Linda Arnold's “There's A Dinosaur Knocking At My Door” has been sung in Jewish preschools from the Bay Area to the U.K. Her first board book brings that beloved song to Shabbat tables everywhere.

The Song

Before it became a board book, this joyful Shabbat story lived as a song sung by families, teachers, and children for decades. This video shares the song that inspired the book.
